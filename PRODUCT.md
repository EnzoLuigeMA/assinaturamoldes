# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Brazilian women who sew ("costureiras"), reached on a phone from a paid Meta ad — often
in the evening, curious but unsure where they fit. They span beginners sewing as a hobby
and an escape, intermediates chasing professional results, and women building a side
income or their own atelier/brand. The job to be done: in under a minute, learn "is this
for me, and which plan?" — and feel understood while doing it.

## Product Purpose
`/quiz` is a gamified, AIDA-structured entry funnel for **Assinatura de Moldes** — Thaíza
Gonçalves's subscription that delivers professional sewing patterns (moldes) every month.
Six quick questions feed **Claude Haiku**, which returns a personalized seamstress
*archetype* + compatibility score + recommended plan. It exists to convert cold ad traffic
by trading a generic pitch for a personal, playful diagnosis, then handing off to the sales
page (`/v5#planos`) with the right plan pre-highlighted. Success = quiz completion + Lead
event + click-through to the recommended plan.

## Brand Personality
Artisanal, feminine, premium yet welcoming. Voice: warm, encouraging, plain-spoken
Brazilian Portuguese — a generous mentor at the sewing table, never corporate or hype-y.
Three physical-object words: **hand-marked, warm, precise** (tailor's chalk on dark fabric,
spools of thread, the dashed cutting lines of a paper pattern). The experience should make
her feel seen and gently guided, with a small thrill of progress and a payoff reveal.

## Anti-references
- The "tasteful AI default": cream/beige background + Playfair-italic display + Inter body.
- Generic SaaS funnel UIs: purple/violet gradients, glass cards, big-number metric dashboards.
- Cold high-fashion minimalism that reads exclusive or intimidating — this brand is inclusive.
- Cheap/arcade gamification (confetti spam, neon). Playfulness stays tasteful and warm.

## Design Principles
- **Make her feel seen, not sold.** Each screen speaks to her situation before a plan is named.
- **Earn the reveal.** Pace the six questions so the archetype result lands as a deserved payoff.
- **Tactile over flat.** Carry warmth through craft materials — thread, chalk, fabric, lamplight —
  not generic UI chrome.
- **One decision per screen.** It's a guided path, not a form; keep cognitive load low.
- **The handoff is part of the product.** The result must lead cleanly to the right plan on
  `/v5`, never dead-end.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast on the dark surface (≥4.5:1 body, ≥3:1 large/bold). Full keyboard operation
with a visible coral focus ring. `prefers-reduced-motion` honored (crossfade / instant in
place of movement). Touch targets ≥44px. Mobile-first single column — most traffic is phone.
